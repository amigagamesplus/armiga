### Fixed

- X and Y buttons were swapped on the Anbernic RG40XX H: the top button was reported as Y and the left one as X. Now the top button is X and the left one is Y, as printed on the console, in the menus and in RetroArch.

### Changed

- The gamepad now uses a driver written specifically for ARMIGA. Sticks, D-pad, buttons, the MODE button and rumble work as before.
- The gamepad configuration used by RetroArch is now refreshed at every boot if it differs from the one shipped with the system, so fixes reach existing installations without resetting the data partition.
- The kernel was updated to 7.3-rc7.
- Amiga Catalog: the play time is now centered in its card.
- The progress bar shown while holding the Dev Mode combo (SELECT+START+L1) now sits higher on the screen.
- Factory reset now also resets the LED settings, and its confirmation explains that the console will reboot and that games and saves are kept.
- Graphics drivers (Mesa) updated to 26.2.5.
- The system update screen was redesigned: it now shows the status of each step, the elapsed time, and pauses on errors before continuing the boot. You will see it the next time you update from this version.
